Step 1 · Read

A fast-growing state

Lesson 13 ended in 1945 with Texans moving from farms to cities. That move never stopped. Texas has grown faster than almost any other state, and in 2025 it added more people than any other state, about 391,000 in one year. More than 31 million people now live here.

Texas population in millions. U.S. Census counts, plus the Census Bureau's 2025 estimate.

Why people come

Jobseconomy

Energy, health care, technology, shipping, and farming. Texas has led the nation in exports for many years, trading heavily with Mexico through its border ports.

Costpull

Housing has often cost less than in states like California, and Texas has no state income tax.

Familynatural growth

Many new Texans are born here. Others move from other states or other countries to join family.

Texas in the world: science and technology

SpaceNASA Houston

Johnson Space Center in Houston has run Mission Control since the 1960s, guiding the Apollo Moon landings and today's astronauts.

ComputersDell · TI

Jack Kilby of Texas Instruments in Dallas helped invent the integrated circuit (microchip) in 1958. Michael Dell started Dell in his UT Austin dorm room in 1984; it is based in Round Rock and sells around the world.

MedicineTexas Medical Center

Dr. Michael DeBakey of Houston pioneered heart surgery and devices that help failing hearts pump. The Texas Medical Center is among the largest medical centers in the world.

The challenges of growth

Waterthe big one

The 2022 State Water Plan projected that Texas's population would grow more than 70 percent between 2020 and 2070, while existing water supplies would fall by about 18 percent as reservoirs fill with silt and aquifers are pumped down. In a severe drought, about a quarter of Texans could face city water shortages without new projects.

Traffic and housingcities

Roads and housing struggle to keep up in fast-growing areas like Austin, Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, and San Antonio.

Landenvironment

Farms and ranchland are paved for suburbs. Growth puts pressure on wildlife habitat and on the Gulf Coast.

Many cultures, one state. Texas has no single majority group. Hispanic Texans and white Texans are the two largest groups, with large Black and Asian American communities and people from nearly every country. You can hear it in the music (Tejano, country, blues, hip-hop), taste it in the food, and see it in festivals from Juneteenth to Diez y Seis to Wurstfest.

How to make a good decision (SE 23B)

  1. Identify the situation that needs a decision.
  2. Gather information.
  3. Identify options.
  4. Predict consequences of each option: pros and cons.
  5. Take action to carry out the decision.
  6. Evaluate how well it worked.
